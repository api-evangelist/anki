# Anki

> Anki, Inc. was a consumer robotics and artificial-intelligence company (San Francisco, founded 2010, backed by Andreessen Horowitz) known for the Cozmo and Vector companion robots and the Anki Drive / Overdrive robotic racing system. Anki ceased operations in May 2019; its assets were subsequently acquired by Digital Dream Labs. Anki published first-party Python and C# SDKs that let developers program its robots' computer vision, navigation, animation, and AI over a local connection. This entry is generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the catalog; the company's original developer host (developer.anki.com) no longer resolves.
